## Medical notes

- Stereognosis is tactile object recognition with eyes closed. It depends on intact primary afferent sensory pathways and parietal cortical processing.
- Astereognosis should not be diagnosed when primary sensation or motor ability is significantly impaired. The original task explicitly states preserved strength and general sensation, which supports cortical sensory localization.
- Difficulty recognizing objects with the right hand localizes most strongly to the left parietal cortex, especially the postcentral/somatosensory association region.
- Spatial orientation complaints and distorted perception of object size/proportion support involvement of parietal association networks, but the lateralizing clue in this case is the right hand.
- The title/source framing suggests a possible parietal neoplasm, but the station data alone should be phrased as suspected focal left parietal lesion until MRI clarifies the cause.
