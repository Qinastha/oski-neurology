## Medical notes

- The safest station answer is not just "back pain", but a left-sided lumbosacral radicular syndrome.
- Exact root localization depends on the exam pattern:
  - L5: dorsum of foot/great toe sensory involvement, extensor hallucis longus or ankle dorsiflexion weakness, possible medial hamstring reflex change.
  - S1: posterior leg/lateral or plantar foot symptoms, plantarflexion weakness, reduced Achilles reflex.
- Because the original task text does not provide complete reflex/myotome findings, the answer should say "L5/S1 left, уточнити за дерматомом, міотомом і рефлексами" rather than force one root without evidence.
- Red flags must be asked explicitly, especially bladder/bowel dysfunction and saddle anesthesia.
