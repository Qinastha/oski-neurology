## Medical notes

- Mini-Cog is a screening test. It supports the need for further assessment but does not establish dementia etiology by itself.
- With progressive memory, language, visuospatial and behavioral symptoms over about one year, the safest OSCE formulation is: progressive cognitive impairment / probable major neurocognitive disorder, with etiology to clarify.
- Required follow-up: collateral history from relatives, functional and safety assessment, labs for reversible causes, depression screening when appropriate, and brain MRI/CT.
