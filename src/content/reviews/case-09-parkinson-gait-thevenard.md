## Medical notes

- The station corresponds to a retropulsion / pull test for postural stability. It must be performed with clear warning, enough room for retropulsive steps and the examiner ready to catch the patient.
- MDS-UPDRS gait assessment includes walking away and back, turning, stride amplitude, speed, foot lift, heel strike and arm swing; freezing should be assessed separately while walking and turning.
- In the pull test, recovery in 1-2 steps is usually normal; 3 or more steps is abnormal. Four backward steps in the scenario supports postural instability.
- The expected OSCE answer is Hoehn-Yahr stage III: bilateral Parkinson disease with postural instability while the patient remains physically independent.
- Caveat: some scale descriptions reserve stronger stage III wording for needing help to recover from pull test. In this station, recurrent falls plus pathological retropulsion and independent walking support the intended stage III answer.
- Management should not stop at "increase levodopa": review wearing-off and medication state, assess orthostatic hypotension and other fall contributors, refer for Parkinson-specific physiotherapy/occupational therapy and fall-prevention planning.
